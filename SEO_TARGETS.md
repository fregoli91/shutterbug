# Shutterbug SEO Targets

The goal is qualified camera-shopping traffic and sales, not a high count of loosely relevant keywords.

## Branded

Examples: `Shutterbug Camera Shop`, `Shutterbug cameras`, `Shutterbug Camera Shop Amazon`.
Primary pages: homepage, About, `/amazon`, Contact.
Measure: branded impressions, sitelinks, correct canonical host, and clicks to shop/Amazon.

## Commercial

Examples: `used cameras`, `used cameras online`, `buy used cameras`, `used camera store`, `vintage camera shop`.
Primary pages: homepage, `/shop`, `/categories/vintage-cameras`.
Measure: non-branded impressions, clicks, product views, cart additions, and transactions.

## Category

Examples: `vintage digital cameras`, `used film cameras`, `compact digital cameras`, `point and shoot cameras`, `CCD cameras`, `used DSLR cameras`, `used camera lenses`.
Primary pages: priority `/categories/[slug]` routes.
Measure: page-level impressions, category-to-product clicks, index coverage, and inventory-supported conversions.

## Brand

Examples: `used Canon cameras`, `Olympus cameras`, `used Nikon cameras`, `Sony cameras`, `Polaroid cameras`, `Kodak cameras`.
Primary pages: priority `/brands/[slug]` routes.
Measure: which brands gain impressions, brand-page product clicks, and model-family query growth.

## Model Family

Examples: `Canon PowerShot`, `Canon Digital ELPH`, `Nikon Coolpix`, `Olympus Stylus`, `Olympus mju`, `Sony Cyber-shot`, `Kodak EasyShare`, `Fujifilm FinePix`, `Panasonic Lumix`.
Primary pages: existing justified model-family category pages and exact product listings.
Measure: exact-family impressions and clicks; add more family content only when inventory or original knowledge supports it.

## Informational

Examples: `how to buy a used camera`, `what is a CCD camera`, `35mm camera buying guide`, `what to check before buying an old camera`.
Primary pages: `/guides` and individual guides, with contextual links to categories and brands.
Measure: assisted product visits, category clicks, engaged sessions, and follow-on branded searches.

## Local

Examples: `used cameras Michigan`, `camera shop Michigan`, `vintage cameras Michigan`.
Primary page: `/used-cameras-michigan`.
Measure: Michigan impressions, contact actions, trade-in interest, and ecommerce sales. Avoid fake city pages.

## Sell / Trade-In

Examples: `sell my camera`, `sell used camera`, `sell vintage camera`, `where to sell old cameras`, `camera trade in`.
Primary page: `/sell-your-camera`.
Measure: qualified quote starts, completed inquiries, accepted inventory, and brand/model mix.

## Practical Search Console Method

1. Compare the last 28 days with the previous 28 days, then review a three-month view for trend stability.
2. Filter out branded queries to judge whether Google is associating Shutterbug with used-camera topics.
3. Sort queries by impressions and identify average positions 8-30; improve the existing relevant page before creating a new one.
4. For queries already ranking well but receiving few clicks, review title, description, availability, and whether the intent matches.
5. Group page results by category, brand, product, guide, local, and sell intent.
6. Track the brands and model families Google is beginning to associate with Shutterbug.
7. Use exact model impressions to guide inventory sourcing and product-title completeness.
8. Record changes and wait long enough to measure them instead of rewriting pages weekly.

## Guardrails

- No fake inventory, specifications, ratings, reviews, testing claims, or product identifiers.
- No indexable arbitrary filter combinations.
- No mass-generated city, model, or keyword pages.
- No use of `refurbished` unless the item was genuinely refurbished and the listing explains by whom.
- No guide expansion without useful original knowledge or clear search/inventory demand.
