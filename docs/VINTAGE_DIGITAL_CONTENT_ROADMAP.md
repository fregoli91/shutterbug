# Vintage digital content roadmap

The evergreen Y2K guide is the central informational resource. Future content should answer a distinct question and link to that guide plus the most relevant commercial page. Avoid publishing multiple articles that repeat “best Y2K cameras” with slightly different wording.

## Priority 1: ownership and compatibility

1. **Vintage digital camera memory-card guide** — Compare SD and early capacity limits, CompactFlash, Memory Stick, xD Picture Card, MultiMediaCard, readers, and safe formatting. Link to vintage digital, accessories, and the Y2K guide.
2. **How to find the right battery and charger for an old digicam** — Explain model-number matching, proprietary packs, AA options, docks, aging cells, and safe replacements. Link to batteries and chargers.
3. **How to transfer photos from a 2000s camera** — Cover card readers, legacy USB cables, docks, file organization, and non-destructive workflows without promising universal compatibility.

## Priority 2: model-family research

4. **Canon PowerShot family guide** — Distinguish Digital ELPH/SD, A, G, and SX families and link to the existing PowerShot commercial page and Canon editorial article.
5. **Nikon Coolpix naming and buyer guide** — Explain pocket, bridge, and rugged families, with storage and battery checks.
6. **Sony Cyber-shot and Memory Stick guide** — Cover common model lines, media variants, batteries, and cable considerations.
7. **Olympus Stylus, mju, and Tough digital guide** — Explain xD storage and why older weather sealing should not be assumed waterproof.
8. **Kodak EasyShare cameras, docks, and batteries** — Clarify charging and transfer setups across common generations.
9. **Fujifilm FinePix storage and power guide** — Cover xD/SD and AA/proprietary battery differences.

## Priority 3: condition and photographic use

10. **Compact-camera lens errors explained** — Describe common symptoms, what a listing can test, and when a camera belongs in parts/repair inventory.
11. **CCD vs CMOS in older digital cameras** — Expand the existing guide with sourced model examples, while avoiding aesthetic guarantees.
12. **Direct-flash photography with an older compact camera** — Practical exposure, distance, red-eye, battery, and etiquette advice.
13. **How to read an old digital camera listing** — Actual-item photos, LCD wear, corrosion, card writing, lens movement, flash, battery, charger, and included accessories.
14. **Caring for a vintage digicam** — Storage, battery removal, humidity, gentle cleaning, cable strain, and data backup.

## Publishing standards

- Choose one primary intent and one canonical URL for every article.
- Use real model families and verified specifications; cite manufacturer manuals where compatibility details matter.
- Link to the Y2K guide, one relevant commercial category, and one supporting guide or journal post.
- Add original photographs or diagrams when available and write literal, useful alt text.
- Keep purchase guidance conditional on the exact listing and never imply that age, CCD, or a model name guarantees an aesthetic.
- Review articles annually or when inventory and compatibility advice materially change.
