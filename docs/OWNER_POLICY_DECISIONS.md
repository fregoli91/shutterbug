# Owner Policy Decisions

These items require an owner decision before the storefront can publish specific promises. The site should continue using cautious, non-specific language until each decision is supplied and implemented.

## Returns

**Decision needed**

- Return window measured from delivery.
- Eligible item conditions and required packaging/accessories.
- Who pays return shipping for preference returns, inaccurate listings, and defective-on-arrival items.
- Whether any restocking fee applies and under what exact conditions.
- Inspection and refund-processing timeline after a return arrives.
- Exceptions for final-sale and parts/repair items.

**Current site behavior**

Product copy states only that returns depend on the item arriving as described or matching its testing notes. The site does not promise a window, prepaid label, restocking fee, or refund timing.

**Recommended options**

1. Publish one standard window with seller-paid return shipping for inaccurate or defective items and buyer-paid shipping for preference returns.
2. Publish category-specific rules for cameras, printers, accessories, and parts/repair items.
3. Keep returns limited to materially inaccurate listings, with clear pre-purchase disclosure and an owner-approved exception process.

**Code/content affected**

Policy and FAQ pages; product-detail return notes; checkout disclosure; order and return emails; customer-support scripts; structured merchant policy if later added.

## Warranty

**Decision needed**

- Whether Shutterbug provides a warranty in addition to the return policy.
- Warranty duration and start date.
- Covered failures, excluded damage, consumables, batteries, and cosmetic issues.
- Whether the remedy is repair, parts, replacement, store credit, refund, or owner choice.
- Rules for parts/repair and as-is inventory.

**Current site behavior**

The storefront makes no general warranty promise. Product pages describe testing and exact-item condition, but testing is not presented as a warranty.

**Recommended options**

1. Offer a short functional warranty for eligible tested items with explicit exclusions.
2. Offer category-specific coverage where testing and repair capacity support it.
3. Provide no separate warranty and rely on the approved return policy, stated plainly before purchase.

**Code/content affected**

Policy and FAQ pages; product badges and detail copy; checkout disclosure; order emails; support workflows; promotional artwork and metadata containing warranty language.

## Shipping

**Decision needed**

- Handling time, business-day cutoff, and holiday rules.
- Supported carriers and service levels.
- Free-shipping eligibility, if any.
- Insurance and signature thresholds.
- Oversized-printer packing, surcharge, local-pickup, and freight rules.
- Damaged-in-transit reporting window, evidence requirements, and claim ownership.
- International destinations, exclusions, duties, taxes, and returns.

**Current site behavior**

The site says items ship from Shutterbug with protective packing. It does not promise a dispatch time, carrier, free shipping, delivery date, insurance, signature, oversized-printer method, or international availability.

**Recommended options**

1. Publish one domestic handling standard with carrier/service chosen per order, plus a separate oversized-item policy.
2. Show item-specific shipping methods and costs only after inventory measurements and checkout rates are reliable.
3. Limit launch shipping to owner-approved domestic regions and add international service later.

**Code/content affected**

Shipping and FAQ pages; product shipping notes; bag and checkout; shipping-rate integration; confirmation and tracking emails; oversized-printer listings; promotional artwork and metadata.

## Trade-ins

**Decision needed**

- Who pays return shipping when inspection leads to a rejected or revised offer.
- How long a customer has to accept or decline an offer.
- Payout methods and timing after acceptance.
- Store-credit value and whether a credit bonus exists.
- Local handoff location, hours, appointment rules, identity checks, and custody receipt.
- Rules for abandoned submissions, prohibited items, data-bearing devices, and unclaimed gear.

**Current site behavior**

Customers can submit authenticated drafts, upload photos, review offers, and accept or decline. Admin revisions are auditable. The site does not promise payout timing, a credit bonus, free return shipping, or a local-handoff process.

**Recommended options**

1. Seller-paid return shipping when Shutterbug changes an offer due to an evaluation error; customer-paid return shipping when disclosed condition materially differs.
2. A fixed acceptance period and fixed payout target measured from acceptance and final verification.
3. Local handoff only after a verified location, staffed hours, appointment flow, and receipt process are approved.

**Code/content affected**

Trade-in landing, submission, offer, and history pages; admin workflow; offer and status emails; terms; payout integration; local-handoff copy; campaign artwork.

## Privacy vendors and tracking

**Decision needed**

The production vendors and purposes for payments, media storage, database hosting, transactional email, analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, support communications, retention, and privacy requests.

**Current site behavior**

The privacy copy is intentionally general and has not been reconciled with final production vendors or tracking choices.

**Recommended options**

1. Use essential providers only and list every production processor and retention purpose.
2. Add analytics without advertising profiles and document consent requirements.
3. Add advertising tools only after owner and legal review of consent, disclosure, and deletion workflows.

**Code/content affected**

Privacy and cookie pages; consent UI; analytics and pixel integrations; forms; account deletion/export workflows; vendor configuration.

## Model-watch alerts

**Decision needed**

Whether model watches should send email, text, or in-account alerts; the trigger, frequency, opt-in, unsubscribe, and duplicate-notification rules.

**Current site behavior**

Customers can save models and remove them from their account. The UI explicitly says automatic email or text alerts are not active.

**Recommended options**

1. Launch with in-account watchlists only.
2. Add opt-in email alerts when a matching active, in-stock listing is published.
3. Add text alerts only after consent, quiet-hour, provider, and compliance rules are approved.

**Code/content affected**

Watchlist and account pages; product publishing workflow; notification jobs and templates; consent records; unsubscribe controls; privacy copy.
